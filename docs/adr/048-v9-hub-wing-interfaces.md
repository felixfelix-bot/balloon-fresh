## 1. Context — what was missing

The v9 flight board is a **3D assembly**: one central hub PCB plus **four
identical wing PCBs** soldered onto the hub's edges at **90° spacing**
(ADR-046 §1). The **wing** side was already fixed in copper
(`tracker/hardware/wing_board/wing_board_v9.kicad_pcb`, 4-pin tab, ADR-046 §2)
and the **hub**-side requirement had been written down
(`docs/WING-TO-HUB-SOCKET-SPEC.md`), but the **hub carried no wing interface at
all**: the v9 design-intent schematic generated by `build_flight_sch.py v9` had
27 components / 35 nets, and the whole 4-wing solar array arrived as a
**1-pad `SOLAR_IN` net** carrying nothing but the BAT54 anode end — filed as
open item `OPEN-12` ("the 4-wing × 3-cell solar array input connector is not in
the v9 component list"). There was no socket, no stack wiring and no bypass
provision on the hub.

This ADR records the hub side as now implemented.

Implemented as components `J_W1`, `J_W2`, `J_W3`, `J_W4` on the v9 sheet, in
one dedicated sheet column, and as four placements of the footprint

Stack order, cited verbatim from ADR-046 §2.3 / socket spec §3.1:

```
stack_top  = W1_SOLAR_P  -> BAT54 -> supercap bank -> TPS7A02 (ADR-006)
             W1_SOLAR_N == W2_SOLAR_P   (hub link)
             W2_SOLAR_N == W3_SOLAR_P   (hub link)
             W3_SOLAR_N == W4_SOLAR_P   (hub link)

```

- **Pad 2 (`GND`) of every interface ties directly to the hub ground plane and is
  NOT in the solar string; no `W<n>_SOLAR_N` is tied to GND except
  `W4_SOLAR_N`.** Cited: socket spec §3, §3.1.
- **Pad 3 (`W<n>_RF`, `RF_FEED`) carries NO net on v9 and is not tied to GND.**
  Cited: ADR-046 §2.4 ("the wing's pin 3 (RF_FEED) is a copper land that is left
  electrically unconnected (a provision)"; "Do not tie it to GND" in socket spec
  §3.2). The pad **name** `RF_FEED` is ADR-046 §2.1's pin name; only the *v9 net
  assignment* is "none".
- **Bypass provisioning:** one Schottky per interface **across
  `W<n>_SOLAR_P` ↔ `W<n>_SOLAR_N`, cathode to `W<n>_SOLAR_P`, DNP for the first
  prototype**, implemented as `D_BP1..D_BP4` (BAT54 family, SOD-323). Cited:
  ADR-046 §2.3 and §6, socket spec §5 ("Package suggestion: SOD-323 or SMA
  (BAT54 family, matching ADR-006's existing BAT54)"). The series-blocking BAT54
  at the stack output (D1, ADR-006) is **separate** and is not replaced.

## 3. Conflict register — exactly what disagreed, and what was implemented

The instruction for this work was: *if the socket spec and ADR-046 disagree on a
number, implement the ADR and record the discrepancy; do not silently pick one.*
Two disagreements were found, plus one internal inconsistency **inside** the
socket spec (not an ADR-vs-spec disagreement).

**D-1 — copper-to-board-edge clearance: 0.5 mm vs 1.0 mm.**
`docs/WING-TO-HUB-SOCKET-SPEC.md` §2 (table row `Copper-to-board-edge clearance`)
and §4 (`Board-edge clearance`) both say **≥ 0.5 mm** for the hub land.
`docs/adr/046-wing-board-interface.md` §2.2 says **≥ 1.0 mm on every tab pad**.
**Implemented: the ADR's ≥ 1.0 mm**, i.e. the land row's near edge sits 1.0 mm
inboard of the interface edge and the land centre sits 3.0 mm from it, so the
lands span 1.0 … 5.0 mm, inside the cited 6.0 mm insertion depth. 1.0 mm is also
the more conservative of the two. The socket spec's 0.5 mm is **not** implemented
and this line is the record of that.

**D-2 — tab/land-row width: 8 mm vs 9.0 mm.**
`socket spec §4` ("Slot-to-slot") reasons *"on a 22 × 22 mm hub an **8 mm-wide
tab** centred on a 22 mm edge leaves 7 mm per side"*. ADR-046 §2.1 and §4.1 fix
the tab at **9.0 mm** (= 4 × 1.2 mm lands + 3 × 0.6 mm gaps + 2 × 1.2 mm side
clearance). **Implemented: the ADR's 9.0 mm.** The derived numbers change with it:
(22 − 9.0)/2 = **6.5 mm** per side instead of 7 mm. The spec's *conclusion*
("no interference") still holds at 9.0 mm, so nothing else in the spec is
invalidated — but the spec's arithmetic was done at 8 mm and its conclusion must
be read at 6.5 mm.

**Internal inconsistency (b) — inside the socket spec, land row offset.**
`socket spec §2` gives the land row's along-insertion offset twice and
incompatibly in the *same table row*: `Land centre offset from the hub's outer
edge | 1.0 mm inboard` vs the parenthetical `lands span 0.0…4.0 mm from the
edge`. A 4.0 mm land cannot be centred 1.0 mm from a datum and span 0.0…4.0 mm
from it; the two readings differ by **1.0 mm**. **Resolved by taking the ADR's
clear number** (the ≥ 1.0 mm clearance of §2.2), which puts the land's inboard
edge — not its centre — 1.0 mm inboard. This is recorded as
`TODO(unverified)` in §5 item 1 rather than asserted as the truth.

**Not a disagreement (checked, no action).** The two records **agree** on: land
4.0 × 1.2 mm; pitch 1.8 mm; 4 lands per face / 8 per interface; pad order 1
SOLAR_P / 2 GND / 3 RF / 4 SOLAR_N; 6.0 mm insertion depth; slot 0.9 mm
±0.10 mm; 4 interfaces at 90°; every GND land on the hub ground plane and no
`W<n>_SOLAR_N` on GND except `W4_SOLAR_N`; the DNP bypass Schottky per
interface; and "the RF land carries no net on v9".

## 6. Consequences

- The hub now carries the four interfaces, the hub-side series stack and the
  four DNP bypass provisions; the v9 sheet's 1-pad `SOLAR_IN` gap (`OPEN-12`) is
  closed by these four interfaces. What is **not** closed is the wing-side
  array-coverage re-budget of ADR-046 §2.3 (the 6.0 V / 2.4 W ADR-006 figure
  only holds with all four wings illuminated).
- The schematic records **nets**, not copper: the land geometry, keep-outs and
  silkscreen live in the footprint
  `tracker/hardware/footprints/tracker_mechanical.pretty/Wing_Tab_4P.kicad_mod`,
  not in a generic pin header, and there is **no v9 hub PCB yet** to place it on.
  The four placements at 90° spacing are therefore recorded here and in the
  sheet's wing column, and remain a board-layout action.
- ERC error count moves 24 → 28, entirely the four unnetted RF provision lands.

## Correction (2026-10-08, `fix/record-contradictions`) — §5 item 6 / sheet `OPEN-23` is RESOLVED

**§5 item 6 (the assembly orientation of the wing at the hub) is no longer open.
The wing plane is VERTICAL (a blade): long axis radial in the hub plane and
pointing outward, 25 mm width vertical.** The governing record is
`docs/adr/049-wing-architecture.md` §5 item 6 (2026-10-07), which resolves it by
arithmetic and says so explicitly ("*Wing plane orientation is VERTICAL … it is
resolved by arithmetic*"). The arithmetic is the tab/slot fit that ADR-046 §4.1
and this ADR's §2.2 both state: a **0.9 mm** slot admits a **0.6 mm** tab plus a
**0.30 mm** nominal gap, and a **9.0 mm** tab cannot fit a **6.0 mm** in-plane
slot — so the wing plane cannot be coplanar with the hub. It is independently
confirmed by **ADR-046 §4.1** ("90° to the hub plane, wing plane normal to the
hub plane"), by **ADR-051** §1.4 and by **ADR-055** §1 (both derive the four
vertical blades), and by `docs/analysis/wing-insolation-geometry.md` §0.

**Nothing in §2 of this ADR changes.** The datum (§2.7), the land row, the slot
and the keep-outs are unaffected, and the footprint still **asserts no rotation**
— as §5 item 6 required. This correction closes the *contradiction*, not the
footprint decision.

The stale reading is `docs/WING-TO-HUB-SOCKET-SPEC.md` §1 ("Wings 1–2 horizontal,
3–4 inclined ≈30° below the hub plane", and its self-contradicting "long axis is
perpendicular to the hub plane"), which is v1-era prose from
`docs/hardware-design.md` §3D-Assembly where "horizontal" described **antenna**
coverage for a wing that carried a Yagi; the wing antenna is V2-only and absent
on v9 (ADR-046 §2.4). That section now carries an appended correction, as does
this ADR's own restatement of the inherited **22 × 22 mm** hub outline in §2.1 /
§2.2 / §2.4 — the current v9 hub outline is **103.0 × 103.0 mm**
(`tracker/hardware/hub_board_v9.kicad_pcb`, S0 placement;
`tracker/hardware/placement-source-of-truth.json`).

**What stays open:** the ±30° droop of wings 3–4 (no v9 rationale, no v9 record)
and the rest of §5 (items 1–5, 7–9) are unchanged. Both resolved values are

## 1. Context — the measured cost fact

The v9 hub outline is currently **103 × 103 mm** (ADR-055 D5), drawn from the 106.1 cm² full-duty
array requirement (ADR-051 §1.4). A live JLCPCB quote-page run (2026-10-07/08, qty 5, 4-layer,
1.6 mm, HASL-with-lead, single PCB, fresh isolated page load per config) located a **size-tier price
discontinuity**:

| largest single dimension | class | total (qty 5) | mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ **102.0 mm** (89 / 100 / 101 / 102 measured) | CHEAP | **$8.00** | a single **"Special Offer $8.00"** line; no Engineering fee |
| ≥ **102.5 mm** (102.5 / 102.9 / 103 measured) | EXPENSIVE | **$31.50 – $31.60** | **Engineering fee $25.00 + Board $6.60**; no Special Offer |

- **Delta: $31.60 − $8.00 = $23.60 per qty-5 order.**
- **The driver is the LARGEST SINGLE DIMENSION**, not area or perimeter: `102 × 102` (area 10404 mm²)
  is cheap while `100 × 104` (area 10400 mm², *smaller*) is expensive; `100 × 104` and `102 × 102`
  share the same perimeter (408 mm) yet land in different classes; `50 × 103` (area 5150 mm²,
  perimeter 306 mm) is expensive.
- **The mechanism is EMPIRICALLY LOCATED, not documented vendor policy.** The page states no
  102/102.5 mm rule. Do not present it as documented. (Measured at the endpoints, reproducible: see §4.)
- The v9 hub at **103 mm sits exactly one millimetre** outside the cheap tier (102 mm).

## 2. Decision

### D1 — The hub outline moves from **103 × 103 mm** to **102 × 102 mm**.

- Area: **106.09 cm² → 104.04 cm²** = **98.07 % retained** (a 1 mm trim on each side).
- Saving: **$23.60** on a qty-5, 4-layer order (the class boundary crossed by 1 mm).
- The full measured basis, the confound, the invalid configurations and the re-verification commands
  are in `docs/analysis/jlcpcb-pricing-and-size-tier.md`. This record does not restate them.

### D2 — The 89 × 89 mm alternative is REJECTED.

It buys the **same** $23.60 (also inside the cheap class) but shrinks the array to 79.21 cm²,
**forfeiting 25.3 %** of the array area. On a saving-per-cm² basis the 1 mm trim is ~13× better.
**89 mm is dominated**; it is recorded here so it is not re-proposed.

### D3 — This is a PRICING-driven outline. The array AREA requirement is NOT redefined here, and it WINS on conflict.

- **This record changes the outline to buy a price class. It does not set the array area.** The
  array area required for full hub-only duty is **106.1 cm²** and that figure, together with the
  operator's sustained-duty choice, is owned by **ADR-055 D3**.
- Harvestable day-mean energy scales **linearly with installed area** (ADR-055 D3). The trimmed
  board's 104.04 cm² is **98.07 % of 106.1 cm²**, so it can hold **≈ 98.1 % of the full-design
  sustained duty** — equivalently, the full 0.388 W design is **~1.9 % short**.
- **Stated explicitly, and not to be smoothed over: if the operator's sustained-duty choice needs the
  full 106.1 cm², then 102 mm and the cheap tier cannot both be satisfied — the array requirement
  wins and this ADR is superseded on that point.** The trim is conditional on the duty decision
  tolerating a ~1.9 % area shortfall.
- **A future session must not let this pricing record silently redefine the array requirement.**

### D4 — No board artefact is changed here, but the trim RE-OPENS hub PLACEMENT (a re-place).

This record changes no `.kicad_pcb`, schematic, netlist or footprint. However, the hub **was placed
and frozen at 103 mm**, and a 102 mm outline **re-opens placement**: the ADR-030 deterministic
placement gate must be **re-run** against 102 × 102 mm and the frozen placement hash does not carry.
**Recorded as a required re-place, not a silent follow-on.**

## 3. Consequences

### Positive
- The board moves from the expensive class into the flat **$8.00** cheap class — **−$23.60** at qty 5.
- The saving is bought for **~1.9 %** of area rather than the **25.3 %** the 89 mm route costs.
- The cost evidence is now durable and reproducible **inside the repo** (raw JSON committed), so it
  cannot be re-argued from memory.

### Costs (accepted)
- **~1.9 % of array area is given up** (106.09 → 104.04 cm²), which is a real, if small, duty cost
  (D3).
- **A re-place is required** (D4).
- The decision is contingent on the duty choice (D3) and on the thickness question (follow-up (b)/(c)).

## 4. The evidence, its status, and its confound (summary; full detail in the analysis doc)

- **Reproducible, not noise:** 103 × 103 measured **four** times → $31.60 each; 102 × 102 measured
  **twice** → $8.00 each; 89 × 89 measured **twice** → $8.00 each; all on fresh isolated loads.
  (`repeats_consistent = YES`)
- **The cheap-class `engineering_fee: null` is real, not a parse failure:** the cheap class renders a
  single "Special Offer $8.00" line and no Engineering-fee/Board lines; both classes reconcile to
  their totals exactly from their own charge lines. **The absence of the fee line IS the mechanism.**
- **The size tier is NOT documented vendor policy** — it is observed at the endpoints only (§1).
- **UNRESOLVED CONFOUND — thickness vs surface finish.** On this vendor the two are coupled: every
  4-layer **0.8 mm** row came back **LeadFree HASL** (Surface Finish charged $5.20–$5.30) and every
  4-layer **1.6 mm** row came back **HASL(with lead)** (Surface Finish $0.00), e.g. 89 × 89 / 4L:
  **$13.20 (0.8 mm) vs $8.00 (1.6 mm)**. **A thickness price effect cannot be separated from a finish
  effect with this data. No thickness price effect is claimed.** What may be said: 0.8 mm costs
  ≈ +$5.20–$5.30 at matched size/layers **and** changes the finish.
- **Invalid configurations (never to be quoted):** `c_89_2L_06`, `c_100_2L_06`, `h_89_2L_16`,
  `h_100_2L_16`, `i_89_4L_04_ENIG` (no usable quote / options failed), plus the residual-schema
  `t1_103_4L_06` (see §5(a)). Listed in the analysis doc §9.
- Prices are a **2026-10-07/08 snapshot**; re-run before any order.

Engineering challenges (why CHANGES_REQUESTED, not APPROVED):
1. Simple-span gravity formula vs continuous carrier are NOT equivalent support
   conditions: continuous support can introduce reaction moments, peel, contact-
   edge stress, local curvature absent from the calibration.
2. "Continuous support is conservative" overstates — defensible wording is
   "no span credit is conservative for the idealized global transverse bending
   calculation, but not a demonstrated conservative bound for the supported
   assembly" (local stress concentrations, imposed curvature from carrier
   flatness, DTE, adhesive peel/shear, residual stress, point/line contact).
3. 27 MPa is a UNIAXIAL fully-restrained estimate, not a universal thermal
   upper bound: equal-biaxial plane-stress gives ~E*alpha*|dT|/(1-nu) ≈ 37.6 MPa
   for nu=0.28, before anisotropy/carrier CTE/adhesive/residual effects.
4. The second dotted line (~35 MPa = E=170 GPa) was unlabelled in the original
   figure — needs explicit label + derivation or removal.
5. 2 g transverse is illustrative only, NOT a validated dynamic envelope
   (no shock, vibration, resonance, fatigue, combined thermal+mechanical).
6. k=0 vertical static must not be read as zero stress in a real assembly
   (multi-axis acceleration, rotating hub, flexible line).
7. Nothing measured: fracture strength, post-bond strength, cold-soak,
   thermal-cycle, vibration, support-edge concentration, residual, 2 g/10 g.

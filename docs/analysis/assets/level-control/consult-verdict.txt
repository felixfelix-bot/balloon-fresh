| F3 | **DSA 31.5 vs 31.75 dB inconsistency.** | **FIXED** — the two are **different parts**: ADL5240 DSA range **31.5 dB** (datasheet); PE43711 **0–31.75 dB** (datasheet). Stated explicitly; 31.75 gives 66.75 dB, indistinguishable from 66.5. |

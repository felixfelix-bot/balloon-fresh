--- TOOL DEFECTS FOUND AND FIXED IN MY OWN GATE ---
1. NESTED-ZIP: most packages wrap the gerbers in a single top-level directory.
   The first version read the wrong level and reported every layer MISSING.
   4 correct packages were falsely flagged. Fixed by descending into the sole
   top-level directory. (v2_adc_4layer_final_jlcpcb, v2_adc_v3, v_c3_4layer,
   and gerbers_v2_adc_4layer_final were all false positives.)
2. DRILL FLOOR: the first version compared holes to VIAS only. A board with
   zero vias but through-hole pads legitimately emits a drill file, so this
   falsely flagged gerbers_v_c3_final and gerbers_v_c3 (both 26 holes against a
   board with 26 drilled pads and 0 vias). Fixed: the floor is
   (vias + drilled pads).

LESSON GENERALISED: I nearly reported 4 phantom defects. A gate that has not
been counter-tested against KNOWN-GOOD inputs will manufacture false alarms, and
a false alarm is as damaging as a missed one -- it teaches the operator to ignore
the gate. A gate must be validated BOTH ways: it must fail the artifact you know
is broken AND pass the artifact you know is correct.

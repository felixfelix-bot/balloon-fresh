## Headline answers

1. **The trade, 650 km / FLRC 2.6 Mbps / `G_balloon = 0 dBi`:** +13 dBm → **27.9 dBi →
   7.38 m** dish (42.8 m², ~163 kg, 53 kN·m at 20 m/s); +22 dBm → 18.9 dBi → 2.62 m;
   **+33 dBm (F33) → 7.9 dBi → 0.74 m.** At 650 kbps: 3.49 m / 1.24 m / 0.35 m.
2. **Is 20 dB of balloon TX cheaper than the dish it removes? YES, decisively.** It removes
   a factor **10** in diameter and **100** in reflector area, and the machine it removes is
   not purchasable (no vendor sells a 433 mesh dish above 1.9 m today), not holdable (14.5×
   the strongest BOM rotator's holding torque even as coarse mesh) and ~163 kg.
   Balloon-side price: **+2.8 g** module (4.0 g F33 vs 1.2 g bare LR2021) and **5.50 W**
   TX DC @ 5.0 V (**6.15 W** @ 5.5 V, already provisioned by ADR-047).
3. **But the binding gate is not mass — it is the licence.** The whole +13…+33 dBm class
   exceeds the licence-exempt **+12.15 dBm EIRP** cap (ADR-039/041). The F33 makes the 433
   downlink amateur-band by design. Also flagged: the repo's own
   `docs/PAYLOAD-WEIGHT-ESTIMATES.md` §D classes the F33 board *"ground station only"*
   (~20.6 g whole board) — the radio change here is a **module swap (+2.8 g)**, and the
   contradiction is named, not silently resolved.
4. **Mesh:** λ/10 = **69.2 mm** at 433 MHz, so commodity 6–25 mm welded mesh is electrically
   solid (the vendor's 6 mm mesh is rated to 6 GHz = 1.20×λ/10 there, and 2.8 mm to
   11 GHz = 1.03×λ/10 — the rule corroborated at its own limit). Wind moment vs the fetched
   **SPID BIG-RAS 2,712 N·m** brake rating at 120 km/h: 2.40 m / 2.62 m as **SOLID** =
   **1.88× / 2.45×** (infeasible); as **6 mm mesh** = **0.50× / 0.65×** (comfortable);
   3.00 m mesh = **0.97× (AT the rating, zero margin)**; 3.49 m mesh = 1.53× (needs the
   slew class). `Cd = 1.38` is **derived** from the Gibertini OP100SE vendor wind figure.
5. **Rate adaptation** is a range/robustness tool, not the answer to the 650 km question once
   the F33 is in play: at +33 dBm a **1.24 m** dish gives *either* 650 kbps at 650 km *or*
   2.6 Mbps at 1,254 km; 1.9 m gives 1,667 km @ 2.6 Mbps. Without the F33 the max-rate
   requirement sets a **2.62 m** dish at **zero margin**.
6. **Recommendation:** put the **2 W F33** on the balloon (REC-1); ground dish
   **1.2–1.9 m coarse mesh** on an SPX-01 for margin/horizon (REC-2); any 433 dish ≥ 2 m
   **must be mesh** (REC-3); if the F33 is excluded, fall back to **+22 dBm + 1.24 m mesh +
   650 kbps at 650 km** (REC-5). No procurement.

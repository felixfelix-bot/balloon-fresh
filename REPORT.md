## The substantive findings

1. **The 103 × 103 mm board could not hold its own array.** Only **2 LARGE cells** (78.55 × 38.90 mm)
   tile in a 103 mm square = **61.11 cm² = 58 %** of the 106.1 cm² target, while the area minimum is
   **4 LARGE cells** (needing 157.1 × 77.8 mm). ADR-055 D5 was internally inconsistent; the
   operator's decision resolves it.
2. **Overhang buys duty margin, not just board saving.** Decoupled, 4 LARGE = 122.2 cm² = **115 %**
   full duty; 6 = 183.3 cm² = 173 %. Conditional on the charge path (ADR-051 §2.2: 61.2 cm²
   face-on already 2.26× the bq25570's 510 mW abs-max).
3. **No sourceable allowable for the unsupported span.** The repo has the cell's geometry, areal
   mass and Si modulus (170 GPa) but **no flexural strength**. Demand derived instead:
   **78.55 mm span @1 g = 0.504 MPa / 18.1 µm sag; @2 g = 1.007 MPa / 36 µm; cantilever = 4×σ /
   9.6×δ = 2.01 MPa / 174 µm.** Closes with one coupon test → `S_crack` → `pitch ≤ S_crack`.
4. **Component floor is measured:** 2582 mm² (25.82 cm²) → **≈ 60 × 60 mm (36.0 cm²)** at 72 %
   packing. **66 % area cut, 5.96 g of 0.4 mm FR4 saved** vs the 103 mm board.
5. **Price tier:** measured cliff at largest side 102 mm → 103 mm 4-layer is **$31.60** vs 102 mm
   **$8.00**. A 60–70 mm outline is deep inside the cheap class.
6. **Thickness lever re-quantified:** 4.514 g → **1.532 g**. ADR-055 D4 amended, not reversed.
7. **Recommended arrangement:** cells on the **spine-and-ribs** carrier (ADR-049 pattern),
   **unbonded** (ADR-052), overhanging **two opposite edges** (not four — all-four puts both ends of
   the outer cells uncarried = the cantilever case). Mass 12.460 g vs 19.523 g (0.6 mm full carrier)
   / 15.009 g (0.4 mm full carrier).

## Verified claims from the task brief

* **Isolation:** CONFIRMED 35/36 carry `page_load` = FRESH/new-tab; `t1` has no `page_load`.
* **Boundary at 102/102.5 mm:** CONFIRMED.
* **Driver = largest side:** CONFIRMED and strengthened — `100x104` has *smaller* area than
  `102x102` yet is priced higher (kills area), and `100x104` shares the *same* perimeter as
  `102x102` in a different tier while `50x103` has *smaller* perimeter and is priced higher
  (kills perimeter).
* **No vendor-stated cause:** CONFIRMED — the page tooltip only documents 0.4 mm/ENIG, 0.6 mm
  <=100x100, 0.8–1.0 mm <=300x300. The boundary is empirically located but **unexplained**; none
  was invented.
* **Repeats:** CONFIRMED.
* **`engineering_fee: null` in the cheap class is not a parse failure:** CONFIRMED — cheap class
  shows a single "Special Offer" line; expensive class shows Engineering fee + Board; both reconcile
  to their totals exactly.
* **Thickness/finish confound:** CONFIRMED — 0.8 mm rows returned LeadFree HASL (charged $5.20–5.30)
  and 1.6 mm rows HASL(with lead) (free); the two effects are **not separable** in this data, so no
  thickness-only delta is reported.
* **Invalid configs listed, not dropped:** DONE — separate labelled table with per-file reasons.
* **Different Design = 2:** REPORTED — DD=2 forces "Panel by Customer" + $16.54 Panel charge and
  pulls the small board out of the promo class; combined order wins once per-order shipping
  ($23.21) is counted, loses if it is ignored.

## New finding not in the brief

* The four "empty price" 2-layer configs have an **empty** `calculated_price` string while
  `SUMMARY.json` records a complete `$4.00` "Special Offer" line — the two on-disk artifacts
  disagree. Treated as **INDICATIVE**, not measured. A cheap re-run would close this.
* Crossing the boundary is not a size premium: it **drops the order out of the promo class**, and
  the **$25.00 engineering fee (a per-order charge) is the dominant term** of the delta — so the
  delta will not scale linearly with quantity. Only qty 5 was measured.

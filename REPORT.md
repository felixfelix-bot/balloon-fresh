## 1. What changed, and why

The F33 land pattern's pad **POSITIONS** were already verified against the vendor's own Altium
pad file. The pad **SIZE was the only unverified parameter** — a declared guess
(`LAND_LENGTH_MM = 2.0`, `LAND_WIDTH_MM = 1.0`) carrying a `TODO(unverified)` flag.

**The operator closed that unknown by reading the vendor drawing.** That is the authoritative
datum for this change:

> **Castellation hole diameter D = 0.80 ± 0.10 mm** — operator's own words: *"the diameter of
> the hole that the pin goes through … 0.8 ± 0.1 mm"*, reading §9 "Mechanism Dimension", p.8 of
> `docs/f33-module/LoRa2021F33-2G4-datasheet-v1.1.pdf` (an embedded JPEG raster with **no text
> layer** — which is why it could not be extracted in-repo).

The module is **castellated**, so the 0.80 mm half-hole presents **0.80 mm of copper along the
edge** and penetrates only **D/2 = 0.40 mm inward** from the module edge (y = ±10.5).

### The derivation

**Rule (one line):** *the land is the castellation aperture grown by a uniform 0.25 mm
solder-fillet margin on every side.*

| axis | derivation | result |
|---|---|---|
| along the edge (**x**) | `D + 2×0.25 = 0.80 + 0.50` | **1.30 mm** (was 2.0 mm = **2.5×D**) |
| across the edge (**y**) | `D + 2×0.25 = 0.80 + 0.50`, symmetric (pad centre pinned on the module edge, must not move) | **1.30 mm** (was 1.0 mm) |

* **Gap between neighbouring lands** = pitch − land = `3.9289 − 1.30 = **2.6289 mm**` (no bridging).
* **Inward** = **0.65 mm** ≥ `D/2 = 0.40` ✓ *and* **Outward** = **0.65 mm** ≥ inward ✓ — outward is
  the **design driver**: every joint on this vehicle is **hand-soldered**, so the iron tip needs
  the castellation accessible from outside; 0.65 mm clears a 0.5 mm chisel tip and is +0.15 mm
  more exposed copper than the old guess (0.50 mm).
* Tolerance band is rule-independent: `D=0.70 → 1.20 mm / gap 2.7289`; `D=0.90 → 1.40 mm / gap 2.5289`.

### `land_before → land_after`
`2.0 × 1.0 mm` (guess, flagged) → **`1.30 × 1.30 mm`** (derived from the operator datum).
The `TODO(unverified)` **land-size flag is dropped** in the generator, the emitted footprint(s)
and the docs. **No other `TODO` was touched.**
